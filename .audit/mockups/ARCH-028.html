Detach captures one full-buffer TerminalScreenSnapshot (chars + fore/back colors, history + screen); the detached window renders and selects from that single structure.

Why it matters beyond the pixels: selection-to-text currently exists 3× (live buffer / viewport fallback / detached string[]) and snapshot building 2× — each with its own clamp/pad/TrimEnd rules, plus a copy-pasted CombineHash. One snapshot structure deletes the parallel builder and BuildDetachedSelectionText; the color fix falls out as a side effect.
