<objective>
Wire PresetManager to JobStorageService so that preset rename, delete, and folder delete operations maintain job reference integrity.

Purpose: Without this integration, renaming or deleting a preset silently breaks all jobs that reference it. This is the #1 pitfall identified in research. Jobs would show "preset not found" errors at execution time with no way to trace why.

Output: Extended PresetManager with job-aware rename/delete flows, and dedicated integration tests.
</objective>
